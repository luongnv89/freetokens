Brand Identity

A curated, self-verified index of currently-claimable free AI token and credit offers. Static, git-managed, zero backend.

Design Concept

The token

A hexagonal chip — the shape developers already read as a token, a credit, a unit of API spend. Six flat sides keep it stable at any rotation and crisp at 16 px.

The spark

The bolt at the centre is the claim: instant, free, no card. It is the only solid mass in the mark, so it survives every downscale.

The inner ring

A second, deeper-green hex echoes the outer edge — the offer inside the token. It is the one layer the favicon drops, keeping the 16 px mark to two readable shapes.
